Opera, in a hurry

Carmen

A soldier falls for a factory worker who answers to nobody. He does not cope with it.

A note: The story ends with a man killing a woman who has left him. Our version keeps that brief and does not joke about it.

The plot in 30 seconds

Don José, a soldier in Seville, is engaged to Micaëla but falls for Carmen, who works in the cigarette factory and loves who she likes. He lets her escape arrest, goes to jail, then deserts to join her band of smugglers. When she tires of him for the bullfighter Escamillo, José cannot accept it, and the opera ends in tragedy outside the bullring.

Who is who

  • Carmen: factory worker, free spirit
  • Don José: soldier, engaged to Micaëla, then obsessed
  • Micaëla: his fiancée, from home
  • Escamillo: bullfighter, the crowd favourite
  • The smugglers: Carmen's friends and José's new crowd

Tunes you already know

The Habanera

Carmen's entrance in Act 1, one of the best-known tunes in opera.

The Toreador Song

Escamillo's entrance in Act 2. You have heard it in adverts and cartoons.

Background

A four-act opera by the French composer Georges Bizet, with a libretto by Henri Meilhac and Ludovic Halévy, based on a novella by Prosper Mérimée. It premiered at the Opéra-Comique in Paris on 3 March 1875, where its realism and tragic ending shocked audiences. It is set in Seville.
